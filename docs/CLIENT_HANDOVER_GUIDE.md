# Client Handover Guide — Crackers King Store Operations

Welcome to **Crackers King**! This simple guide explains how to operate your crackers store website and admin dashboard.

---

## 🛍️ 1. Customer Experience Overview
- **No Login Needed**: Customers open the site, browse festive products, search, add items to cart/wishlist, and submit order requests without creating an account.
- **Order Confirmation & WhatsApp**: Once submitted, customers receive a unique Order Reference ID (e.g. `CK-240826-58921`) and can click **Send Order on WhatsApp** to message your store directly.
- **Order Tracking**: Customers can check their live order progress anytime under **My Orders** or by typing their Order Reference ID.

---

## 🔐 2. Admin Dashboard Operations

### Logging In
1. Navigate to `/admin` or `/admin/login` on your computer or phone.
2. Enter your admin email and password.

### Managing Products & Stock
1. Click **Products** in the admin sidebar or navigation.
2. **Add Product**: Click **Add New Product**, fill in the name, category, MRP price, selling price, and stock quantity. (The discount percentage `% OFF` is calculated automatically!).
3. **Update Stock**: Click **Edit Product** on any item to update stock quantities.

### Managing Orders & Realtime Alerts
1. When a new customer places an order request, a **NEW ORDER RECEIVED** popup alert will appear automatically inside your admin panel.
2. Click **View Details** to review customer name, phone, address, and ordered items.
3. Click **Confirm & Message Customer** to confirm the order and open WhatsApp to message the customer.
4. Click **Print Receipt** to open the printable A4 invoice receipt for transport packing.
5. If an order is cancelled, select **Cancelled (Restore Stock)** to automatically restore ordered quantities back into product stock.

### Managing Promotional Banners
1. Click **Banners** in the admin sidebar.
2. Click **Add New Banner** to upload offer images, titles, and sequence numbers.
