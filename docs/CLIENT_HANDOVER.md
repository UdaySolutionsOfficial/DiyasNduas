# Crackers King — Store Owner Operational Guide

Welcome to **Crackers King**! This guide explains how to operate your crackers store website and admin control panel in simple, non-technical steps.

---

## 🛍️ 1. Customer Shopping & Order Flow
- **No Account Required**: Customers visit your website, browse festive crackers, search products, add items to cart or wishlist, and submit order requests without creating an account.
- **Unique Order Reference**: Every order receives a unique ID (e.g. `CK-240826-58921`). Customers can click **Send Order on WhatsApp** to message your store directly.
- **Order Tracking**: Customers can check live progress anytime under **My Orders** or by searching their Order ID on the website.

---

## 🔐 2. Admin Dashboard Operations

### Logging In
1. Go to `/admin` or `/admin/login` on your computer or phone.
2. Log in with your store administrator email and password.

### Managing Products & Stock
1. Click **Products** in the navigation menu.
2. **Add Product**: Click **Add New Product**, fill in product name, category, original MRP price, selling price, and available stock quantity. (The discount percentage `% OFF` is calculated automatically!).
3. **Update Stock**: Click **Edit** on any item to increase or decrease stock.

### Managing Realtime Orders
1. When a customer places an order request, a **NEW ORDER RECEIVED** popup alert will appear automatically in your admin panel with a chime sound.
2. Click **View Details** to review customer name, phone number, address, and ordered items.
3. Click **Confirm & Message Customer** to verify the order and message the customer on WhatsApp.
4. Click **Print Receipt** to open the printable A4 transport invoice for box packaging.
5. If an order is cancelled, select **Cancelled (Restore Stock)** to automatically restore ordered quantities back into your product inventory.

### Store Controls & Settings
1. Click **Settings** in the admin sidebar.
2. **Store Open / Closed**: Toggle your store open or closed.
3. **Accepting Orders On / Off**: Toggle order taking. When turned OFF, customers can browse your catalog but checkout is safely paused.
4. **Update Contact Details**: Change your store support phone number, WhatsApp number, email, address, and business hours anytime.

### Hero Banners & Announcements
1. Click **Banners** to add, edit, or sequence homepage hero offer banners. Use the visual preview box to see how the banner looks on mobile and desktop.
2. Click **Notices** to create top announcement messages (e.g., *"🎉 Early bird festive crackers booking open!"*).
