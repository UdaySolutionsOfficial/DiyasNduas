# Client Legal & Business Compliance Checklist

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **LEGAL DISCLAIMER**: This software platform provides technical e-commerce capabilities. The client/business owner must independently verify all applicable local, state, and national laws, licenses, permits, tax obligations, storage regulations, transportation restrictions, and sale conditions before operating the website for commercial orders.

## Required Business Verification Items

1. **Firecrackers Sale & Transport Licensing**:
   - Obtain necessary explosive licenses, local municipal permits, and state transport permits for Sivakasi/local firecrackers distribution.
2. **GST & Tax Compliance**:
   - Ensure proper GST registration and invoicing compliance for all crackers transactions.
3. **Consumer Protection & Age Verification**:
   - Verify local legal age requirements for fireworks purchasing and display relevant safety disclaimers.
4. **Order Terms & Privacy Policy**:
   - Provide client-approved legal text for Store Terms of Service, Shipping Policy, Cancellation Policy, and Privacy Policy.
